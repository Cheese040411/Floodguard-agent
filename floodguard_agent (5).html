Disaster Safety Guides

Standard operating guidelines for Malaysia

Flash Flood Protocol

1. Turn off main electrical switches and gas valves.
2. Pack 'Beg Kecemasan' with IC, essential medication, and power banks.
3. Evacuate safely to designated PPS (Pusat Pemindahan Sementara) when instructed by NADMA.

Severe Thunderstorm & Winds

1. Move away from glass windows and tall metallic structures.
2. Secure outdoor items prone to becoming projectiles during Sumatra squalls.
3. Pull over in a safe, open area if driving during torrential downpours.

Hazardous Haze / API > 200

1. Restrict outdoor physical activities and wear N95 respirators outdoors.
2. Keep windows tightly shut; operate HEPA air purifiers indoors.
3. Stay hydrated and monitor official API reports via Department of Environment (DOE).

FloodGuard Agent Ecosystem

Transitioning from static conversational chatbots to an autonomous agentic ecosystem that observes multimodal disaster feeds (IoT water sensors, damage photos, citizen SOS), computes severity via reasoning cores, and autonomously executes multi-step relief actions across Malaysian districts with cross-district backup logistics.

SDG 11
Sustainable Cities & Communities

Building resilient river basins and urban corridors against monsoon flash floods and infrastructural disruptions.

SDG 13
Climate Action

Deploying real-time automated emergency broadcasting, GPS route optimization, and relief inventory allocation without human latency.

Context & Pain Point

During monsoon seasons and climate emergencies in Malaysia, emergency responders face severe bottlenecks. When local district inventories or rescue vans run short, the agent autonomously queries adjacent district ledgers (e.g., dispatching 15 rescue vans from Ipoh to Sungai Siput) to guarantee zero supply shortages.

Target Audience

At-risk rural and urban communities, district disaster management committees (NADMA / JKM), and frontline emergency responders.

Impact & Urgency

Eliminates human response latency during critical flash flood windows, preventing loss of life and securing timely medical & food aid at PPS centers.

Generative AI Tools & Vibe Coding Integration

Google Gemini & Claude: Used for rapid prompt-to-code architecture (Vibe Coding 101), designing agentic logic loops, and synthesizing emergency SMS templates.
Multimodal Ingestion Engine: Simulates computer vision analysis of disaster site photos and real-time IoT water level telemetry.
Value Added: Reduced prototyping time by 80%, enabling high-fidelity interactive simulation maps (Leaflet + Tailwind) without traditional boilerplate coding.
